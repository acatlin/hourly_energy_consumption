Load is in megawatts (MW), one reading per hour, as published by PJM for the AEP zone. Timestamps are local clock time as published.

Summer is June through August and winter is December through February. A month counts as above average when its mean exceeds the mean of every hourly reading. Weekend is Saturday and Sunday.

Data: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/acatlin/hourly_energy_consumption/main/AEP_hourly.csv, one of twelve per-zone files from the Kaggle dataset Hourly Energy Consumption (PJM Interconnection).
